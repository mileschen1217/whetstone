## Constraints

- Page format and the owner's line enter this plugin on the owner's ruling, not on a harness Δ; the real-project runs behind the ruling are recorded in `evals/BASELINE.md` and kept as private material, and while a unit measures them they are a `Where: live` criterion with an evidence file; when the live target is not met, the sentences the owner ruled stay and the target moves to the backlog entry whose epic can decide it. A rule elsewhere in a skill, one whose sentence decides in or out for the agent, still needs its case. check: none. from: two-layers read-before-signing B-3
- The tag on a decision line and on a ship row is the author's own word; no check, script or reader backs it, so a wrong tag moves an item below the line without a signal. check: none. from: two-layers line B-4
